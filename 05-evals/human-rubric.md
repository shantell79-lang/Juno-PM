## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Creation of a dashboard the provides a list of risks, ranked by, high, medium, and low, and provide recommendations to mitigate each risk.
- **Value proposition:** Provide a plan to mitigate the risk of customer cancellations and improve customer experience.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Is the mitigation plan able to be implemented based only on the information provided? | Yes / No | Not enough or accurate information provided | n/a | All information to implement the mitigation is available.  |
| Data Driven | Able to tie the data back to the original data source | Yes / No | If any of the data is made up or not accurate. | n/a | The data provided is real and can be tied back to the original course data. |
| Information Availability | Does the dashboard have all the required data | 1 to 5 | The dashboard was not provided. | The dashboard was generated and some of the expected data was available. | The dashboard was generated, and all of the expected data was available. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 answers every time the dashboard is created
- **Who scores them with you:** Me plus 2 other PM's
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** For any scores that differ, we will review to determine what was incorrect and update the instructions accordingly.

## Pass bar

If enough data is available that I can understand and action what is needed to mitigate the risk.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Any data that is made up will  be an automatic fail.
